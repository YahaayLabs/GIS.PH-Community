# Code of conduct (short)

GIS.PH Community is for constructive, inclusive conversation about Philippine geospatial data and the GIS.PH platform.

## Expected

- Assume good intent; disagree on ideas, not people
- Help newcomers; prefer clear, searchable answers
- Credit sources and respect data licenses

## Not allowed

- Harassment, hate, or personal attacks
- Spam, unsolicited promotion, or scraping recruitment
- Sharing secrets, keys, or private user data
- Deliberate misinformation about data sources or outages

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content and restrict accounts that break these rules. Report problems to **support@gis.ph** or by contacting a maintainer on GitHub.
